Controls

  • Move: mouse, finger, or the Left / Right arrow keys (A / D)
  • Launch: click, tap, or press Space
  • Pause: P or Esc
  • Sound: M

Where the ball lands on the paddle sets its angle: hit it near the edge for a sharper shot.

Bricks and power-ups

  • Coloured bricks break in one hit. Higher rows score more.
  • Silver bricks take two or three hits.
  • Dark steel bricks never break.
  • W wider paddle M multi-ball
  • S slower ball +1 extra life
